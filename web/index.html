      How this was made

      The street conditions follow the walkability indicators of Transform Transport's UX Mobility study (Milan, 2025), built on the Walk21 framework. Every one of the city's 14,816 street sections gets a value for nine conditions, ranked against all the others: a typical Barcelona street sits in the middle, the best ones at the top.

      The way shown is the shortest one on the city's street network, so it can include stairs. Walking time assumes 4.8 km an hour. Three conditions are proxies: shade and water (tree canopy, climate shelters and fountains), low traffic (the 2017 road noise map, checked against 246 traffic counts) and pedestrian streets (plazas and sections without traffic noise).
